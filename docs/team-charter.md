## Contingencies

- **Scheduling conflicts:** find a common in-person or online meeting time.
- **Absences:** provide updates remotely and continue assigned deliverables.
- **Work-quality conflicts:** raise concerns with teammates through agreed communication channels.
- **Personal conflicts:** communicate the core issue clearly; escalate to a professor or TA if unresolved after four days.
- **Emergencies:** compromise and redistribute urgent work among remaining teammates.
- **Deadlines/extensions:** hold an emergency virtual meeting to complete deliverables or reassess goals and deadlines.

## Professionalism with Teammates and Stakeholders

- Team members are expected to conduct themselves professionally at all times.
- Conflicts and inquiries should be approached with the same professional tone.
- Constructive feedback should be provided to teammates.
